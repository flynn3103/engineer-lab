Engineer Lab · Internals Playgrounds

Six interactive playgrounds that show how popular systems work inside. Bring your own data or manifest: each page runs a small real engine in your browser and animates every step. No backend, nothing to install.

1Load your dataPaste or upload a CSV / JSON / JSONL (or a Kubernetes manifest) or use a sample.
2See the big pictureAn architecture overview shows every component and how they connect. Click one, or trace a request.
3Go phase by phaseEach phase animates one mechanism with its own controls (failures, tuning, policies).

What these are (and are not)

Real mini-enginesResults come from small implementations running in your browser (parsers, planners, logs, simulators), not canned animations.
Learning modelsSimplified on purpose: scaled timings, reduced protocols, no wire compatibility. Each page notes its limits.
InteractiveChange data, tuning and failure injection, then watch the invariant hold or break.

Part of engineer-lab. Everything runs client-side; your data never leaves the page.
